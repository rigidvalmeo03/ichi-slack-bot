Ichi Slack Bot

This is a slack bot to know more about your furry besties<3. It shows you information about pets, animal chemistry, and pet care. (but only cats and doggies here lol:P)

Features of thiz very cool bot of mine:

1. Cat Chemistry
Learn about chemicals and nutrients related to cats. This is so goated.

2. Dog Chemistry
Learn about chemicals and nutrients related to dogs. Mwehehe.

3. Dog Breeds  
Use this to get information about a randomly selected dog breed. You may even get your doggie's breed by chance! Very fun:3

4. Cat Breeds 
Get information about a randomly selected furry feline cutsie breed.

5. Dog Food Safety  
Check foods that dogs should avoid. Very big nuh uh.

6. Feeding Reminders  
For someone who easily forgets (like me), you can get a daily reminder to feed your pets! 

7. Ping  
This is quite boring buuuuut it checks the bot's response latency!

8. Help  
View all available commands. No cap>,<

THEEE Commands:
- `/ichi-slack-bot-catchem`
- `/ichi-slack-bot-dogchem`
- `/ichi-slack-bot-dogbreed`
- `/ichi-slack-bot-catbreed`
- `/ichi-slack-bot-dogscanteat`
- `/ichi-slack-bot-feeding HH:MM`
- `/ichi-slack-bot-ping`
- `/ichi-slack-bot-help`
